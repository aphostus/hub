> Founded on October 11th of 2026(Apohub), September 10th of 2026(Aphostus), Apohub was virtually established to server Aphostus in its entirety by our works and by our presence.
The goal of Aphostus is to contribute to human technology and to move the people to the next era of knowledge, wisdom, curiosity
and general understanding. More importantly we aim to participate in international commitments like for the United Nations
until forever and to improve international literacy.

Aphostus was founded in response to the founder's observation of low academic literacy within 
the Philippines. As part of its mission, Aphostus is developing an active research initiative to investigate 
the underlying causes of the nation's academic underdevelopment,

a project regarded as its foremost 
priority, given its potential to advance teaching methodologies and support both students and educators through 
proven, effective practices. A parallel initiative focuses on equipping individuals with the skills to apply 
artificial intelligence toward improving international infrastructure and employment, in recognition of the shift 
toward a skills-based global workforce. In summary, Aphostac is an integrated academic social platform, a workspace solution for students and educators, 
an innovation hub, an academy, and a provider of academic products and services united under a single mission: to become. "The Future Global Academic Center."
